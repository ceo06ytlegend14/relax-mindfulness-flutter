## Five generated noises

White, pink, brown, blue and violet noise are independently seeded outputs of FFmpeg's `anoisesrc`, generated by [`tool/prepare_ambient_pack.py`](../../tool/prepare_ambient_pack.py), with **no third-party audio samples**. The project makes these five generated audio files available under **CC0-1.0**. They are labelled generated noise, not nature/field recordings, binaural tracks, therapy or clinically effective sleep/focus treatments. Grey noise remains unavailable rather than mislabelling another noise as perceptually equalized.

The [CC0 1.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en) was fetched when recording this evidence. The canonical terms are <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. CC0 waives the affirmer's copyright/related rights to the extent possible and includes a public-licence fallback; it does not promise rights the affirmer does not own or warrant against third-party claims.

## Editing and technical checks

- Keep at most the first 61 seconds of each source; linearly overlap the final/first second and rotate by one second, yielding up to 60 seconds of continuous material. There is no artificial silent fade-out between repeats.
- Target −24 LUFS / −6 dBTP before lossy encoding, leaving extra headroom for codec overshoot. Encode MP3 at 96 kbps / 44.1 kHz; field recordings stereo, generated noise mono. Do not call these lossless or HD.
- The manifest pins the exact encoder build and output hashes. The reproducible script can download only the verified source blobs; use a scratch output directory when trying a different encoder.
- Automated checks verify distinct bytes, manifest/chooser/catalog identity, recorded hashes/sizes, full MP3 decoding, non-silence and bounded levels. Technical checks are not a human listening review or proof of sample-perfect loops on iOS/Android.

## Remaining boundaries

These **seven recordings + five generated noises** are twelve distinct sources, not twelve additional rain variants and not coverage of every card. The six legacy mixer clips, `calm_guided.mp3`, and unrelated remote streams **do not acquire rights through this manifest**. Their content review remains open. The original Wood Thrush page also contains historical comments about automated music-platform claims; the current page still displays CC0. A source-site grant is evidence, not an indemnity against erroneous Content ID or genuine third-party claims.

Owner/content review, a human listening/loop pass, and signed-device playback/interruptions/background testing remain release gates. No public/store release was made as part of this addition.
