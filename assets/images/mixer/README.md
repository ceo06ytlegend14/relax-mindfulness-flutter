# Mixer cover artwork

## Current sound-card covers (2026-11-19)

The ten photographic-style covers below are **AI-generated decorative artwork** created for this redesign, then center-cropped/resized to 384 × 384 and compressed to WebP. They are not field-recording location photographs, evidence of a new audio file, or stock photographs copied from a source library:

- `soft_rain.webp`, `thunderstorm.webp`, `rain_window.webp`
- `rain_roof.webp`, `rain_umbrella.webp`, `rain_tent.webp`
- `rain_leaves.webp`, `urban_rain.webp`, `heavy_rain.webp`, `light_drizzle.webp`

The common direction was cinematic deep-teal natural scenes, mint highlights and restrained amber light, without people, text, logos or interface elements. `rain_roof` depicts a car roof, consistent with the configured remote source, not a fictional attic recording. The source PNGs were development-only intermediates and are not bundled.

`noise_cover.webp` and `tones_cover.webp` are **original procedural abstract graphics**: seeded teal/bronze gradients, fine grain and concentric elliptical rings. They do not represent a measured waveform or a verified frequency.

All 75 sound cards resolve a local cover through `MixerTrack.coverAsset`. Related cards intentionally share decorative covers; unique cover counts must not be marketed as unique recordings. Selected Nature/Places cards also reuse local player/Sleep artwork; see [player provenance](../player/README.md) and [Sleep provenance](../sleep/README.md). All twelve mixer cover files total about 295 KiB. An unavailable image recovers to a local vector symbol, never an emoji/font placeholder.

## Retained background and release boundary

`moonlit_forest.webp` remains a crop of the design reference supplied by the user. **Permission to redistribute that background is still unconfirmed.** The six previous reference-cropped rain thumbnails were replaced with generated covers; this does not clear other retained reference-derived assets in the project.

Generated imagery is not a guarantee of exclusive ownership or clearance. The owner must review provider terms, branding and every retained asset before a public store release. No audio asset was generated, replaced or newly licensed by this cover update; see the [sound-library audit](../../../docs/SOUND_LIBRARY.md).

All headings, sound cards, buttons, switches, sliders and navigation are interactive widgets, not a screenshot overlay. The MixerSans typeface is Roboto; its SIL Open Font License is bundled at `assets/fonts/mixer/OFL.txt`.
